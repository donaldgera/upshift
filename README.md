# Upshift

Build a Codeforces practice problemset from problems other people have solved and you haven't.

**[Open Upshift](https://upshift.upshift-practice.workers.dev/)**

## How it works

1. Enter **Your handle** and add up to five **Learn from** handles.
2. Choose **Any source** for problems solved by at least one source, or **All sources** for problems solved by every source.
3. Select **Build queue**. Only accepted submissions count, and each problem appears once.

**To solve** contains your unsolved problems; **Already solved** contains those you've completed. The default order is the sources' earliest accepted submission, oldest first. Each problem links to its statement and accepted submissions.

- **Find problems:** search, filter by rating or topic, and change the sort order. The completed list has its own filters.
- **Pick randomly:** set an inclusive minimum and maximum rating, then request a problem or pick another. The picker uses the full unsolved queue and excludes unrated problems.
- **Refresh:** fetch the latest data without losing your settings. The last successful update stays visible; failed refreshes retain previous results. Open pages automatically refresh comparisons older than 24 hours.
- **Customize:** show or hide ratings and tags, and choose System, Light, or Dark appearance.

Settings and your last comparison are saved in your browser. No login is required; preferences do not sync between devices.
